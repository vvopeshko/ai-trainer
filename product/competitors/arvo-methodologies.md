## 1. DC Training (Doggcrapp)
**Author:** Dante Trudel  
**Level:** Advanced  
**Summary:** High-intensity training using rest-pause sets (to failure, 10–15 breaths, repeat), 60–90 second extreme stretching, and low frequency (every 4–8 days). Beat your logbook every session or rotate exercises. Blast for 6–12 weeks, cruise for 10–14 days.

### Sets & Reps
| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Working Sets | 1 |
| Warmup | 2–3 progressive sets |
| Compound Reps | 7–10 |
| Isolation Reps | 7–10 |

### RIR Target
| Phase | RIR |
|---|---|
| Normal | 0 RIR |
| Intense | 0 RIR |
| Deload | 3 RIR |

### Tempo
**3-1-1-1** (Eccentric–Pause–Concentric–Top)  
- Eccentric: 3s | Pause: 1s | Concentric: 1s | Top: 1s

### Training Frequency
- Per Muscle Group: [4–8]x/week

### Session Duration
- 45–60 min

### Progression Strategy
- **Priority:** Reps First  
- **Deload Triggers:** Cannot beat logbook 2+ sessions; joint pain; sleep quality declining; motivation dropping; strength regressing; 6–12 weeks since last cruise

### Volume Guidelines (MEV/MAV/MRV) — sets per week
| Muscle | MEV | MAV | MRV |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chest | 1 | 1 | 2 |

| Triceps | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Shoulders | 1 | 1 | 2 |

## 2. Evidence-Based Progressive Overload
**Author:** Arvo Standard  
**Level:** All Levels  
**Summary:** Methodology-agnostic hypertrophy training based on progressive overload, moderate volume, RIR-based intensity, 2x/week muscle frequency, and double progression. Standard plan: 3 working sets, compound 6–10 reps, isolation 8–15 reps, RIR 1–2.

### Sets & Reps
| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Working Sets | 3 |
| Warmup | 2 progressive |
| Compound Reps | 6–10 |
| Isolation Reps | 8–15 |

### RIR Target
| Phase | RIR |
|---|---|
| Normal | 2 RIR |
| Intense | 1 RIR |
| Deload | 4 RIR |

### Rest Periods
- Compound: 120–180s  
- Isolation: 60–120s

### Tempo
**2-0-1-0** (Eccentric–Pause–Concentric–Top)  
- Eccentric: 2s | Pause: 0s | Concentric: 1s | Top: 0s

### Training Frequency
- Per Muscle Group: 2x/week  
- Pattern: 3–5 days per week

### Session Duration
- 50–70 min

### Progression Strategy
- **Priority:** Reps First  
- **Set Strategy:** Maintain weight, add reps. When all sets hit upper rep range at target RIR → add 1.25 kg (upper body) / 2.5 kg (lower body), drop to lower range.

## 3. FST-7 (Hany Rambod)
**Author:** Hany Rambod  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Summary:** Fascia Stretch Training — 7 consecutive sets with short rest (30–45s) on the final exercise per muscle group to stretch fascia via extreme pump. Combines heavy compound work + high-volume pump work. Used by multiple Mr. Olympia champions.

### Sets & Reps
| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Working Sets | 4 |
| Warmup | 2 progressive (activate muscle + mind-muscle connection) |
| Compound Reps | 8–12 |
| Isolation Reps | 8–12 |

### RIR Target
| Phase | RIR |
|---|---|
| Normal | 1 RIR |
| Intense | 0 RIR |
| Deload | 3 RIR |

### Rest Periods
- Isolation: 60–90s  
- FST-7 sets: 30–45s

### Tempo
**2-1-1-1** (Eccentric–Pause–Concentric–Top)  
- Eccentric: 2s | Pause: 1s | Concentric: 1s | Top: 1s

### Session Duration
- 70–90 min

### Progression Strategy
- **Priority:** Reps First  
- **Deload Triggers:** Unable to complete 7 FST-7 sets with min 8 reps; severe DOMS 5+ days; loss of pump/mind-muscle connection; joint pain; chronic fatigue; performance declining on compound exercises

## 5. Kuba Method
**Author:** Jakub Sylvester-Cielen

|---|---|

| Deload | 3 RIR |

### Rest Periods

- Isolation: 90–120s

### Tempo
**3-1-1-1** (Eccentric–Pause–Concentric–Top)  
- Eccentric: 3s | Pause: 1s | Concentric: 1s | Top: 1s

### Training Frequency

### Progression Strategy

## 9. Wendler 5/3/1
**Author:** Jim Wendler  
**Level:** All Levels  
**Summary:** Simple strength program using 4-week wave cycles. Training Max (TM) = 90% of true 1RM. Train submaximal, hit AMRAPs, add weight slowly. Built for long-term progress with minimal complexity.

### Sets & Reps
| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Working Sets | 3 |
| Warmup | 3 progressive (40%, 50%, 60%) |
| Compound Reps | 1–5 (+AMRAP on final set) |
| Isolation Reps | N/A |

### RIR Target
| Phase | RIR |
|---|---|
| Normal | 0 RIR |
| Intense | 0 RIR |
| Deload | 3 RIR |

### Rest Periods
- Main lifts: as needed  
- Assistance: shorter

### Training Frequency
- Per Muscle Group: 1x/week  
- Pattern: 4 days/week, one main lift per day

### Session Duration
- 45–60 min

### Progression Strategy
- **Priority:** Training Max Increase  
- **Set Strategy:** After completing all prescribed reps, increase Training Max by fixed amount each cycle

### Volume Guidelines (MEV/MAV/MRV) — sets per week

## 11. Y3T (Yoda 3 Training — Neil Hill)
**Author:** Neil Hill  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Summary:** Undulating periodization with 3-week microcycle: Week 1 (Heavy: 6–8 reps, strength focus), Week 2 (Hybrid: 8–12 reps, balanced hypertrophy), Week 3 (Hell: 15–30+ reps, metabolic stress). After 9 weeks — rotate exercises. Used by 4x Mr. Olympia Flex Wheeler.

### Sets & Reps
| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Working Sets | 3 |
| Warmup | 1–2 progressive (Week 1); minimal (Week 3) |
| Compound Reps | 6–12 |
| Isolation Reps | 10–20 |

### RIR Target
| Phase | RIR |
|---|---|
| Normal | 1 RIR |
| Intense | 0 RIR |
| Deload | 3 RIR |

### Tempo
**2-0-1-0** (Eccentric–Pause–Concentric–Top)  
- Eccentric: 2s | Pause: 0s | Concentric: 1s | Top: 0s

### Training Frequency
- Per Muscle Group: 1x/week  
- Pattern: 4-day split or 5-day bodybuilding split

### Session Duration
- 60–80 min

### Progression Strategy
- **Priority:** Cycle Based  
- **Deload Triggers:** Declining Week 1 heavy lifts; unable to complete Week 3 Hell workouts; joint pain; chronic fatigue; illness/DOMS 7+ days; mental burnout
